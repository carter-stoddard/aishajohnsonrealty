Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Your trust matters. This Privacy Policy explains how Aisha Johnson, a licensed REALTOR® and Broker Associate with Trillion Real Estate (DRE #01769136) (“we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us through this website.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

When you complete our contact form or otherwise reach out to us, we may collect:

  • Your first and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Whether you are interested in buying, selling, or both
  • Any notes or details you choose to share about your real estate needs

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we automatically collect certain technical information through cookies and analytics tools (including Google Analytics), such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and how you interact with the site.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide real estate brokerage services
  • To contact you about properties, listings, and opportunities relevant to your request
  • To send updates, market information, or marketing communications (only where permitted)
  • To improve our website and understand how visitors use it
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, and licensing obligations

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only as follows:

  • Brokerage: With Trillion Real Estate, the licensed brokerage under which Aisha Johnson operates, as needed to provide services to you.
  • Service providers: With trusted vendors who help us operate (for example, our email/lead-delivery provider and analytics provider), bound to protect your information.
  • Professional partners: With lenders, escrow, title companies, inspectors, or other professionals only when relevant to a transaction you are pursuing and with your knowledge.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect our rights and safety.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We use Google Analytics, along with cookies and similar technologies, to track and understand website traffic — such as how many people visit the site, which pages they view, and how they found us. This helps us measure performance and improve the site. The data is collected in aggregate and is not used to personally identify you. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; some features of the site may not function properly if you do. To learn how Google uses data, visit Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Your California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) gives you the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how we use and share it
  • Request access to or a copy of your personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal exceptions
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information (we do not sell your information)
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will verify your request before responding.

6. Data Retention & Security

We keep your information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described here or as required by real estate recordkeeping and other legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information, though no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

7. Third-Party Links

Our site may link to third-party websites (such as social media or listing platforms). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

8. Children’s Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
